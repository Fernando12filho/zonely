How the fairness score works

Every half-hour slot gets a pain score from 0 to 100 for each teammate — 0 if it sits inside their working day, 100 if it would wake them up. Hours just outside the workday cost more the further out they go, because the fourth hour past dinner hurts more than the first.

A slot's score is the team's average pain, plus a penalty for being lopsided. That's deliberate: optimising the average alone will happily sacrifice one person every single week to keep everyone else comfortable.

Once you log a meeting, the person who took the hit carries a burden. The next time you plan, Zonely pushes pain toward whoever has carried the least — so the bad slot rotates instead of landing on your Singapore colleague forever.

All times computed with the IANA timezone database, daylight saving included. Nothing is stored — your team lives in the URL.
